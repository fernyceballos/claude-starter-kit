# Ferny's Claude Code starter kit

A one-paste setup for the people Ferny coaches. It gives your Claude Code the same working setup
Ferny uses, tuned to what you want to do:

- **Operator**: Claude works inside your tools for you (a CRM, Slack, email, a website builder),
  in your own browser.
- **Researcher**: Claude studies a profile, videos or a market and tells you what works.
- **Builder**: Claude builds an app, portal or website with code.
- **All three**: a bit of everything, in one folder.

It also teaches you the way of working that keeps Claude sharp: one chat per job, **wind down**
at the end, **continue in new session** when a chat gets full, and nothing goes live without
your yes.

## Getting started

1. Ferny invites your GitHub username to this private repo. Accept the invite (check your email).
2. Make a folder for your first project and open it in Claude Code.
3. Paste the prompt in [BOOTSTRAP.md](BOOTSTRAP.md).

Your setup takes about 20–30 minutes, most of it waiting for installs.

## Later

- **New kind of work?** Make a new folder, open it in Claude Code, and say **start a project**.
- **Grow this folder?** Say **upgrade this project**.
- **Get the newest version:** say **update the kit**. (In the terminal you can also turn on
  auto-update, and setup shows you how. The desktop app updates by hand only.)
- **Forgot how something works?** Say **coach me**, or **show me the guide** for the field guide:
  every phrase, when to say it, and where things are in the app, on one page you keep.
- **Stuck?** Contact ferny@attractionmarketing.com.
