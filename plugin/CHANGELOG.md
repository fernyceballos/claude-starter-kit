# What's new in the starter kit

## 0.1.8 — 2026-10-30

- If you say **wind down** or **continue in new session** in a chat that only did the coach (or
  only talked), Claude now just tells you there's nothing to save, instead of writing notes about
  nothing. In a chat that did real work, both work the same as before.

## 0.1.7 — 2026-10-29

- The coach no longer invites you to type a phrase like **wind down** in the middle of the
  lessons. Lessons you can try on the spot still say **Try it now**. The rest say **Next time**,
  and tell you to save the phrase for a real job, because those phrases do real work (wind down
  would close out the coach chat).

## 0.1.6 — 2026-10-27

- Fixed: after an update in the desktop app, setup, the coach and the guide could open an older
  copy of the kit if the app still keeps one. They now always pick the newest copy.

## 0.1.5 — 2026-10-26

- **New: your field guide.** One page with every phrase you can say, when to say it, the route of
  a job from new chat to wind down, and where things are in the app. Click a phrase to copy it.
  The coach gives it to you as your own private page to keep (you can pin it in your Claude
  sidebar), and **show me the guide** opens it any time.

## 0.1.4 — 2026-10-26

- The coach and setup now say where to see how full a chat is in the desktop app: the
  **Context** percentage in the panel on the right. The meter at the bottom of the screen only
  shows in the Terminal, and the desktop app's number doesn't remind you at 60%, so glance at it.

## 0.1.3 — 2026-10-26

- Setup no longer sends desktop app users looking for an auto-update switch. The app doesn't have
  one, so it explains how to update by hand instead: say **update the kit**, or click Check for
  updates, then Update.
- Corrected clicks for updating the kit and for switching off an old add-on in the desktop app.

## 0.1.2 — 2026-10-26

- Setup now offers **all three** (operator, researcher and builder) as a choice, for when you
  want a bit of everything in one folder.

## 0.1.1 — 2026-10-26

- Setup now works in the Claude desktop app. The app keeps the plugins it installs in its own
  folder, so setup couldn't confirm Superpowers or find the kit after you'd installed them there.
  It now looks in both places.
- The desktop app steps are fixed: Superpowers is added straight from the app's Plugins screen
  (search "superpowers"), and the starter kit's marketplace is added with **+ Add → Add
  marketplace** on that screen.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-26

- First version: setup with three starter kits (operator, researcher, builder), the coach
  walkthrough, and the skills for winding down, handing off a full chat, autopilot, plain-English
  summaries, design styles, backlog reviews, and studying social media profiles and videos.
- A meter at the bottom of the screen shows how full the chat is, and reminds you to start a new
  chat at 60%.
