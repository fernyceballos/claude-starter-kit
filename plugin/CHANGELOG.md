# What's new in the starter kit

- First version: setup with three starter kits (operator, researcher, builder), the coach
  walkthrough, and the skills for winding down, handing off a full chat, autopilot, plain-English
  summaries, design styles, backlog reviews, and studying social media profiles and videos.
- A meter at the bottom of the screen shows how full the chat is, and reminds you to start a new
  chat at 60%.
