# Video 3 — "Bumper sticker" (about 1 minute)

**On screen:** a long, technical answer, then typing "bumper sticker", then the short version.

Sometimes Claude gives you an answer that's way too long, or full of words you don't know.
Here's my favorite fix. Just type two words: **bumper sticker.**

*(Type it.)* Watch. Same facts, short bullets, plain words. Like something you could read on the
back of a car.

Here's a real one. I asked why leads from a new page weren't showing up, and the first answer was
a wall of settings talk. Bumper sticker gave me: "The form works, but it was sending leads to an
old list. It's fixed now. About 40 people landed on the old list. They're safe, and you can move
them in one step." Ten seconds, and I knew exactly what to do.

I use this all the time, and I've been doing this a while. You should never nod along to
something you don't understand. Asking for the short version isn't dumb. It's how you stay in
charge of your own project.

One more thing: the short version still tells you if Claude made a mistake earlier, or if
there's something it hasn't checked. It's shorter, not sugar-coated.

Try it on any long answer: type **bumper sticker**.
