# Video 2 — Talk it through first (about 2 minutes)

**On screen:** starting a new chat with "I'd like help with…" and Claude asking a question.

When you ask Claude for something new, it won't just start building. It'll ask you a few
questions first. That's on purpose, and it's one of the best things about this setup.

Here's why. When I hand a job to a person, the more they understand what I want and *why* I want
it, the more of the little decisions they get right without bugging me. Claude is the same. Ten
minutes of talking it through saves an hour of "no, that's not what I meant."

You don't need fancy words. Answer like you'd answer a friend: "I want new leads to get a
welcome email, because right now they hear nothing from me for a week." That "because" is gold.

*(Show Claude playing back a summary.)* Every so often it'll say back what it understood. Read
that part. If something's off, just say so. That's the cheapest moment to fix it.

And if you're in a hurry and don't want the questions, say "just start." It'll make sensible
choices and tell you what it picked.

Next time you have something you want help with, start the chat with "I'd like help with…"
and see what it asks you.
