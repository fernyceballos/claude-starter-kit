# Video 6 — Claude asks before anything goes live (about 2 minutes)

**On screen:** Claude showing a draft email with "OK to send to these 120 people?"

Your accounts are real: your CRM, your email list, your website. So some things are always your
call. Claude will **always ask you first** before it sends a message to real people, publishes
something, deletes something, or spends money.

*(Show the draft.)* When it asks, it should show you the exact words and exactly who gets them.
If it doesn't, ask: "show me the exact text and who gets it." Then say yes or no.

Your yes counts for that one thing only. If it wants to send a second email, it asks again.
That's not Claude being annoying. That's the safety net working.

You'll also sometimes see a box that says something like **"allow this command?"** That's Claude
asking before it runs something on your computer. Reading or listing files? Usually fine, say
yes. Not sure what it does? Say no and ask Claude to explain it in plain words. You won't break
anything by asking.

Bottom line: Claude does the work, and you hold the keys.
