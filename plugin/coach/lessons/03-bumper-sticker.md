# "Bumper sticker"

## What
If an answer is too long or too techy, reply with two words: **bumper sticker**. Claude says it
again in short, simple bullets.

## Why
You should never have to nod along to something you don't understand. Asking for the short
version is normal. You'll still get the real facts, just without the extra words.

## Try it now
Scroll up to any long answer in this chat, then type **bumper sticker**.
