# Claude asks before anything goes live

## What
Claude will **always ask you first** before it sends a message to real people, publishes
something, deletes something, or spends money. You answer with a clear yes or no.

Sometimes you'll also see a box asking **"allow this command?"** That's Claude asking before it
runs something on your computer.
- If it's reading or listing files, saying yes is usually safe.
- If you're not sure what it does, say no and ask Claude to explain it in plain words.

## Why
Your accounts are real. A message can't be unsent, and a post can't be un-seen. So these stay
your call, every time. A yes covers **that one action only**, not the next one.

## Next time
Next time Claude asks "OK to publish?", read what it's about to do before you say yes. If it
didn't show you the exact words or who gets them, ask it to.
