# "Wind down" at the end of every job

## What
When a job is finished, start your message with **wind down**. Claude writes down what got
done, what's still open, and anything it learned. Then it tidies up.

## Why
A new chat only knows what's in the notes. "Wind down" is how today's work gets into the notes, so
the next chat can pick up where you left off. Without it, Claude may redo finished work or ask
you the same questions again.

It only works when your message **starts** with "wind down". Just talking *about* winding down
doesn't set it off.

## Try it now
At the end of your next real job, type **wind down** and read what Claude writes in the notes.
