# Talk it through first

## What
When you ask for something new, Claude asks you some questions before it builds anything.
**Answer in your own words.** You don't need the "right" words.

## Why
The more Claude understands what you want and why, the more of the small choices it gets right
without asking. Ten minutes of talking saves an hour of redoing.

If you'd rather skip the questions, say **"just start"**. Claude will make sensible choices and
tell you what it picked.

## Next time
Think of one small thing you'd like help with this week. When you start your next chat for it,
begin with "I'd like help with…" and see what Claude asks.
