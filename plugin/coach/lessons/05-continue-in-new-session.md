# "Continue in new session" when the chat gets full

(A "session" is just another word for a chat.)

## What
If the chat is getting full but the job isn't done, start your message with **continue in new
session**. Claude saves everything, writes a note for the next chat, and gives you a short
message to paste into a new chat.

## Why
"Wind down" is for finished work. This one is for work that's half done. It makes sure nothing
gets lost when you switch chats, even things you only said out loud in the chat.

## How you'll know it's time
- **In the desktop app:** the **Context** number on the right side reaches **60%**. It won't
  remind you, so glance at it now and then.
- **In the Terminal:** the bottom of your screen says **"chat 60% full → say continue in new
  session"**.
- Or Claude tells you the chat "just got squeezed to make room".

## Next time
Don't type it now. This chat isn't full, and there's no half-done job to hand over. Just remember
60%. When you see it during a real job, say **continue in new session**, then paste the message
Claude gives you into a new chat.
