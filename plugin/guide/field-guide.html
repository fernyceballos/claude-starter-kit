The five you'll use every day

When a job is finished.

Claude writes down what got done, what's still open, and what it learned, then tidies up. The next chat starts from those notes.

When the chat is about 60% full and the job isn't done.

Claude saves everything and gives you a short message to paste into a new chat, which picks up exactly where this one stopped.

When an answer is too long or too techy.

Claude says it again in short, plain bullets. Same facts, fewer words.

When you'd rather skip Claude's questions.

Claude makes sensible choices, does the work, and lists what it picked so you can change anything.

When you want to learn, or forgot how something works.

A short walkthrough, one lesson at a time. Say continue the coach to pick up where you left off.

The route of one job

Every job runs the same line, from a new chat to a clean finish.

Station 1

Open a new chat

One chat, one job. Claude reads this folder's notes first, so it knows where things stand.

Station 2

Talk it through

Start with and answer Claude's questions in your own words. Ten minutes of talking saves an hour of redoing.

Station 3

Claude does the work

It builds, checks its own work on the real thing, and tells you plainly what it did and didn't check.

Any time

You say yes before anything goes live

Claude always asks before it sends to real people, publishes, deletes or spends money. A yes covers that one action only.

Station 4

The end of the line: pick one

The job is done

Then open a new chat for the next job.

The chat is filling up, the job isn't done

Then paste Claude's message into a new chat.
